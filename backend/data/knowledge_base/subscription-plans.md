# Subscription Plans and Premium Access

## Available plans
StreamNest Basic is free with adverts, 720p quality and 1 screen at a time. StreamNest Plus costs 7.99 USD per month with no adverts, 1080p quality and 2 screens at a time. StreamNest Premium costs 12.99 USD per month with no adverts, 4K HDR quality, 4 screens at a time and offline downloads on up to 6 devices. Annual billing is available for Plus and Premium at the price of 10 months.

## Upgrading your plan
Upgrade at Settings > Plan > Change plan. Upgrades take effect immediately and you are charged a prorated amount for the rest of the current billing period. Your billing date does not change.

## Paid but Premium features are not available
After a successful payment, Premium features usually unlock within 5 minutes. If they do not, first sign out of the app and sign back in, which refreshes your plan status (called an entitlement) on that device. Next, check Settings > Plan to confirm the plan shown is Premium and that you are signed in to the same account you paid with; payments made through Apple or Google only apply to the account that was signed in during purchase. If the plan still shows Basic or Plus after 30 minutes, contact support with your invoice number or app-store order ID so an agent can manually sync the entitlement.

## Downgrading your plan
Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing period, so you keep your current features until then. Downloads above the new plan's device limit are removed when the downgrade takes effect.

## Free trial
New members can try Premium free for 7 days. A payment method is required to start the trial, and the first charge happens on day 8 unless the trial is cancelled before then. Each payment method and household can use the free trial once.
