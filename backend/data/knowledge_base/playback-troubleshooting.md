# Video Playback Troubleshooting and Error Codes

## Buffering or low video quality
StreamNest adjusts quality to your connection speed. HD needs at least 5 Mbps and 4K needs at least 25 Mbps. To improve playback, move closer to the Wi-Fi router or use a wired connection, pause other large downloads, and restart the router. You can set a fixed quality under Settings > Playback > Video quality, but choosing a quality higher than your connection supports causes buffering.

## Error SN-1001: playback failed to start
SN-1001 means the video stream could not be started, usually because of a temporary network problem or outdated app data. Close and reopen the app, then try again. If it persists, clear the app cache (Android: Settings > Apps > StreamNest > Storage > Clear cache; iOS: delete and reinstall the app) and make sure you are using the latest app version.

## Error SN-2003: protected content could not be played
SN-2003 is a content-protection (DRM) error. It appears when the device or browser cannot verify that it is allowed to play protected video. Update the browser or device software, disable screen-recording or screen-mirroring apps, and in browsers make sure "Play protected content" is enabled. 4K content on computers requires a compatible browser and an HDCP 2.2 display connection.

## Error SN-4040: title not available in your region
SN-4040 appears when a title is not licensed in the country StreamNest detects from your network. Turn off any VPN or proxy and restart the app. If you have recently moved country, the catalogue updates automatically after you sign in from the new country.

## Audio and video out of sync
Restart playback first. On smart TVs and soundbars, turn off audio enhancement or surround modes and set the TV's audio output to PCM. If the problem only occurs with one title, report it through the player's "Report a problem" menu so the content team can check that file.
