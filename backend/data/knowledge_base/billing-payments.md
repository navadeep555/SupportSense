# Billing and Payments

## Accepted payment methods
StreamNest accepts Visa, Mastercard and American Express credit or debit cards, PayPal, and UPI in India. App-store subscriptions bought through Apple or Google are billed by those stores, not by StreamNest, so payment changes for them must be made in the App Store or Google Play account settings.

## Billing date and invoices
You are charged on the same calendar day each month as your first paid day. If that day does not exist in a month (for example the 31st), the charge happens on the last day of the month. Invoices are emailed after each successful payment and can be downloaded at any time from Settings > Billing > Invoice history.

## Failed or declined payments
If a payment fails, StreamNest retries automatically after 1, 3 and 7 days. During this 7-day grace period you keep full access to your plan. If all retries fail, the account moves to StreamNest Basic until a valid payment method is added. Common causes are an expired card, insufficient funds, or the bank blocking an international online transaction.

## Charged twice or unexpected charge
A duplicate charge usually appears when a payment was retried while the first attempt was still pending. Pending authorisations normally disappear from your bank statement within 3 to 5 business days without action. If both charges are still marked completed after 5 business days, contact support with the transaction IDs from your bank statement and Invoice history; confirmed duplicate charges are refunded in full to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

## Updating a payment method
Go to Settings > Billing > Payment method and select "Update". A temporary verification hold of up to 1 USD (or local equivalent) may appear and is released by your bank automatically. Updating the payment method does not change your billing date.

## Taxes and currency
Prices are shown in your local currency where supported and include applicable sales tax or VAT. If your bank converts from another currency, it may add a foreign-transaction fee that StreamNest does not control.
