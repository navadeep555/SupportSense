# Supported Devices, Apps and Screen Limits

## Supported devices
StreamNest apps run on iOS and iPadOS 16 or later, Android 10 or later, Samsung and LG smart TVs from 2020 onwards, Android TV and Google TV devices, Amazon Fire TV, and Roku. In a web browser, use the latest version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. Older devices may continue to work but no longer receive updates or support.

## Installing and updating the app
Install StreamNest from your device's official app store. Turn on automatic updates; versions older than 12 months stop working because of security and content-protection changes. If the app will not install, check that the device has at least 500 MB of free storage.

## Too many screens streaming
Each plan allows a fixed number of screens streaming at the same time: 1 on Basic, 2 on Plus and 4 on Premium. If you see "Too many people are using your account", stop playback on another device or upgrade your plan. To sign out devices you do not recognise, use Settings > Security > Sign out of all devices and then change your password.

## Offline downloads
Downloads are available on Premium for phones and tablets only. Each Premium account can keep downloads on up to 6 devices, with a maximum of 100 titles per device. Downloaded titles expire after 30 days, or 48 hours after you start watching, and must be renewed while online.
