# Refund Policy (2024, archived)

## Refund eligibility window
Members could request a full refund within 7 days of a charge for a new subscription, as long as fewer than 5 hours of content had been watched. Renewal charges were not refundable.

## How to request a refund
Refunds were requested by email only, to billing@streamnest.example, including the account email and the last four digits of the card.

## Refund processing time
Approved refunds were returned to the original payment method within 14 business days.
