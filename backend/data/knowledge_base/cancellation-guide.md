# Cancelling or Pausing a Subscription

## How to cancel
Go to Settings > Plan > Cancel subscription and confirm. You keep paid features until the end of the current billing period, after which the account moves to StreamNest Basic. Cancelling does not delete your account, profiles or watch history. App-store subscriptions must be cancelled in the Apple or Google account settings.

## Pausing instead of cancelling
Plus and Premium members can pause their subscription for 1, 2 or 3 months from Settings > Plan > Pause. No charges are made while paused, and the account uses StreamNest Basic during the pause. The subscription resumes automatically on the selected date, and the billing date moves to the resume date.

## Rejoining after cancellation
You can restart a paid plan at any time from Settings > Plan. Profiles, watch history and preferences are kept for 10 months after cancellation. Downloads are not restored and must be downloaded again.

## Cancellation and refunds
Cancelling stops future charges but does not automatically refund past charges. To ask for a refund, see the current Refund Policy.
