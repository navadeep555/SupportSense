# StreamNest General FAQ

## What is StreamNest
StreamNest is a subscription video-streaming service offering films, series and documentaries. Members watch on the web at app.streamnest.example, on iOS and Android phones and tablets, and on supported smart TVs and streaming sticks. A free StreamNest Basic tier with adverts is available, and paid plans remove adverts and unlock higher video quality.

## Contacting support
Support is available by in-app chat and by email at help@streamnest.example. Chat is staffed 24 hours a day for Premium members and from 08:00 to 22:00 (UTC) for other members. Email replies are sent within two business days. To speed things up, include your account email, the device you are using, and any error code shown on screen.

## Supported languages
The StreamNest apps are available in English, Spanish, French, German, Hindi and Portuguese. Change the app language under Settings > Language. Subtitle and audio languages depend on each title; available options appear in the player under the speech-bubble icon.

## Content availability by country
Because of licensing agreements, the catalogue differs between countries. A title available in one country may not appear in another. StreamNest determines your country from your network location. Using a VPN or proxy may cause titles to disappear or trigger error SN-4040.

## Service status
If several features stop working at the same time, check status.streamnest.example for ongoing incidents before troubleshooting your device. Planned maintenance is announced there at least 48 hours in advance and usually happens between 02:00 and 04:00 UTC.
