# Account Access and Login Troubleshooting

## Resetting a forgotten password
On the sign-in screen select "Forgot password" and enter the email address on your account. A reset link valid for 60 minutes is sent to that address. If the email does not arrive within 10 minutes, check your spam or promotions folder and confirm you typed the same email used at sign-up. Each new request invalidates earlier reset links, so always use the most recent email.

## Account locked after failed sign-in attempts
After 5 incorrect password attempts within 15 minutes, StreamNest locks sign-in for that account for 30 minutes to protect against password-guessing attacks. The lock lifts automatically; support agents cannot remove it early. Resetting the password during the lock is allowed and the new password works once the 30 minutes have passed.

## Two-step verification problems
If two-step verification is enabled and you no longer receive codes, use one of the ten backup codes provided when you enabled the feature. If you have lost your backup codes and your phone, contact support. For security, an agent must verify identity with the account email, the last four digits of the payment card on file, and the date of the most recent charge before two-step verification can be disabled.

## Changing the account email address
Go to Settings > Account > Email and enter the new address. A confirmation link is sent to the new address and a notification is sent to the old one. The change takes effect only after the confirmation link is clicked. If you no longer have access to the old address, support can update the email after identity verification.

## Signed out unexpectedly
StreamNest signs out every device when the password is changed, and signs out devices that have not been used for 180 days. If you are repeatedly signed out within minutes, clear the app's cache or browser cookies for streamnest.example and make sure the device date and time are set automatically.
