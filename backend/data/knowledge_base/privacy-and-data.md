# Privacy, Personal Data and Account Deletion

## Downloading your data
You can request a copy of your personal data at Settings > Privacy > Download my data. The export contains your profile details, viewing history, ratings and billing history, and is delivered by email as a download link within 7 days. The link expires after 14 days.

## Deleting your account
Go to Settings > Privacy > Delete account. You must cancel any active paid subscription first. After you confirm, the account is deactivated immediately and permanently deleted after 30 days; during those 30 days you can contact support to restore it. Billing records are kept for 7 years where tax law requires it, even after deletion.

## Viewing history and profiles
Each profile has its own viewing history. To remove a title from history, open Settings > Profiles > Viewing activity and select "Hide". Hidden titles no longer influence recommendations.

## What support agents can see
Support agents can see your account email, plan, billing dates, the last four digits of your card and device sign-in history. Agents never ask for your full card number or your password. Report any message asking for your password to security@streamnest.example.
