# Refund Policy (current)

## Refund eligibility window
Members can request a full refund within 14 days of a charge for a new subscription or an upgrade, provided they have watched fewer than 2 hours of paid-plan content since that charge. Monthly renewal charges are refundable within 48 hours if no content has been watched since the renewal.

## How to request a refund
Request a refund through in-app chat or by email to help@streamnest.example. Include the account email and the invoice number from Settings > Billing > Invoice history. Subscriptions purchased through Apple or Google must be refunded by that app store under its own policy; StreamNest cannot issue those refunds.

## Refund processing time
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Card and PayPal refunds take 5 to 10 business days to appear; UPI refunds usually appear within 3 business days. Access to the paid plan ends when the refund is issued.

## Non-refundable situations
Partial months after the eligibility window are not refunded; instead, the plan stays active until the end of the paid period. Charges older than 14 days, and accounts closed for violating the Terms of Use, are not eligible for refunds.

## Policy changes
This policy replaced the 2024 refund policy on 15 January 2026. Charges made on or after that date follow this policy.
